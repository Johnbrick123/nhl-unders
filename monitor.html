Model and learning

How alerts reach you

  • Every run checks the data (board refreshed, games logged and graded, starting goalies coming through). Problems open a GitHub issue on the repo, which emails you.
  • Accuracy alerts wait for 500 graded team-games (about mid-November) so a bad week can't trigger one.
  • The weekly learner refits the weights every Monday. It changes the board only when new weights beat the current ones on games they weren't fit on, at most once every 4 weeks and by at most 10 points per input. Every change opens an issue saying what changed and why.
  • Issues close themselves when the problem clears. Model-change issues stay open until you close them.
